A personal portfolio for a full stack developer and AI / machine learning student. Actual projects and the owner's story are the evidence; no invented clients, testimonials, or results.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Contact messages are stored locally. Production deployment is deferred. Uploads use Spatie Media Library and FilePond.
